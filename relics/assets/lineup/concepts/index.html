These are earned soccer icons for young players’ profiles and year-end player cards, like trading-card badges. Players will collect these and more throughout the season, and all their earned icons will appear on their player card at year’s end. Each badge matters because it recognizes a moment the kids can remember and feel proud of.

Each of the 34 badges has two generated concepts: one builds on the current image and one offers a different visual direction. These generated options are for review; the original lineup icons remain intact.
