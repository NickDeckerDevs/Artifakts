# Sidelines Pixel Art Badge Art Prompts

## Review context

All 34 live badges were reviewed on the published gallery. These are earned icons for youth players' profiles, like trading-card badges. More icons will be added, and each player's earned icons will appear on their end-of-year player card. The art represents recognition, team moments, effort, and memories that matter to the kids. The suggestions below are art directions only; they do not replace or modify the current assets.

## Shared visual direction

Use this direction with each prompt: Each image is an earned soccer achievement icon for a young player's profile and year-end player card, like a trading-card badge. Create one cheerful, child-friendly badge in crisp, chunky pixel art. Use a bold, simple silhouette and centered square composition built from a few large pixel clusters; keep the main shapes distinct and avoid fine lines, tiny accents, or lettering. Use warm cream and bright lavender as the main colors, with clear contrast against a dark-neutral player-card surface. Export the icon on a transparent background with clean square pixel edges. Make one icon only, not a card mockup. No words, numbers, gradients, realistic textures, or background scenery.

## Match icons

### Assist

- **Build on this image:** Keep the current cream player, lavender pass, and ball. Make the assist unmistakable by showing the passer clearly sending the ball along a short lavender trail toward a second teammate who is ready to score; keep both players and the ball bold and distinct.
- **Alternative image:** Create a simple assist badge with two teammates: one player makes a clear pass and the other receives it with a bright lavender starburst between the ball and the receiver, showing a helpful setup rather than a goal celebration.

### Goal

- **Build on this image:** Preserve the cream outer goal, lavender inner net, and ball crossing the line. Enlarge the ball at the goal mouth and add a compact lavender burst just behind the line so the scoring moment reads immediately.
- **Alternative image:** Show a soccer ball nestled inside a small goal net with a single lavender pennant or sparkle above it, forming a clean, unmistakable goal-scored silhouette.

### Sub

- **Build on this image:** Keep the two player silhouettes and the directional swap arrow. Clarify the substitution by showing one player stepping onto the field as the other exits, with the lavender arrow pointing between the sideline and pitch.
- **Alternative image:** Depict two teammates exchanging a simple lavender player-bib or high-five at the touchline, with a simple field boundary marking the substitution moment.

### Whistle

- **Build on this image:** Retain the cream whistle and lavender sound marks, but add a simple mouthpiece opening and three evenly spaced sound blocks so the object is unmistakably a referee's whistle.
- **Alternative image:** Show a small referee hand holding a whistle with two bold lavender sound waves, centered as a clear match-official badge.
