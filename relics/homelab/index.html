1 · Use the files already in your repo

  • Open this file on your laptop. Keep the neighboring files together.
  • Open Ethernet and Power + USB to review the complete connections.
  • Keep the existing Expo cables identifiable so each returns to its same socket.
  • Use the named wall-plate groups. Choose the final individual jack positions during installation.
  • Internet settings and automatic NAS shutdown are outside this build.

2 · Wall plates and panel

  • Office West: OF-WP-RACK-WW matches OF-WP-ROOM-WW. PC and meepmax1 use J1/J2.
  • Office South: OF-WP-RACK-SW matches OF-WP-ROOM-SW. Apple TV and Samsung use J1/J2. Move the existing South pair into its own rack-side plate; no replacement wall runs.
  • Family room: FR-WP-RACK groups the West equipment area, plant shelf and existing FR-GA cable.
  • B2 guest bedroom: B2-WP-RACK matches B2-WP-ROOM. Two proposed runs, J1/J2.
  • B1 bedroom: B1-WP-RACK matches B1-WP-ROOM. Two proposed runs, J1/J2.
  • Master bedroom: MB-WP-RACK matches MB-WP-ROOM. Two proposed runs, J1/J2.
  • Garage: one existing FR-GA record. Leave parked at panel 17 with no switch jumper. No new garage run.
  • Existing room connections occupy panel 17–24 at the right. New bedrooms occupy 11–16. Leave 1–10 available on the accessible left.
  • Each wall plate has four jack positions. Populate only the assigned jacks.

3 · Rack Ethernet

  • Use one connection at a time in the map.
  • Follow each switch socket, panel socket, rack wall plate, room wall plate and device.
  • NAS: preserve the two existing cables and their exact Expo 2 / 4 connections. No upper/lower reassignment.
  • Keep Orbi at its present location with its present Ethernet path.
  • Connect the remaining rack devices directly to their mapped switch sockets.
  • Raspberry Pi 400 and TRENDnet are future office equipment; their office connections are mapped separately.

4 · Power

  • Wall outlet: UPS original input cord.
  • UPS Battery/Surge B1: NAS original supply.
  • UPS Battery/Surge B2: Tecmojo PDU input cord.
  • PDU 2: AT&T gateway original adapter.
  • PDU 3: Expo original adapter.
  • PDU 4: Runner original AC cord.
  • PDU 5: Q1000K original adapter.
  • PDU 7: Orbi original adapter; keep its existing location.
  • PDU 8: SmartThings original DC adapter.
  • PDU 1 / 6 / 9: available.
  • SONOFF: retain NAS USB data and power connection.
  • Pi 400: future office location, matching USB-C supply at office outlet. PDU shared USB capacity does not establish a dedicated 3 A output.
  • B1/B2 are labels you apply in the marked Battery/Surge bank, not factory socket numbering.

5 · Record what you installed

  • Check each moved cable and its device.
  • Record the actual jack choice in the connection notes.
  • Save the connection record file after installation.
  • Keep original Expo assignments intact; do not change switch settings to match a proposed drawing.
