U3–U718-inch NAS shelf

ASUSTOR NAS

Placement. Screw the included 2U × 18-inch ECHOGEAR vented tray into U3–U4. Stand the NAS on its original feet, drive bays facing you, starting about 1 inch back from the tray’s front. The case is 6.85 × 6.69 × 9.06 inches (W × H × D). With a 1-inch setback, the back of the case is about 10 inches from the front, leaving about 8 inches to the nominal rear edge of the tray before plugs. Measure actual overhead space. Five units give about 2 inches above the case before tray and foot offsets.

Use the ECHOGEAR tray’s supplied backstops where they can catch the chassis without touching the fan, plugs or cables. Around 14 inches from the front is a dry-fit starting point for a loose keeper if the slots allow it. Keep the space behind the fan clear. Leave the separate 20.9-inch-minimum Tecmojo rails on the bench. Use the NAS’s original feet and the pads under the rack feet. Record the NAS’s weight with its drives before selecting any separate NAS vibration pads.

Power

NAS power jack → original brick → UPS battery socket 1. Strap the brick beside the NAS through tray vents and keep it clear of the fan. Guide the AC cord down the left rear post.

USB

Existing SONOFF cable → same NAS USB jack it used before. Leave the other USB sockets and HDMI empty unless you are restoring an existing device.

Ethernet

Upper jack → original MikroTik NAS top jack . Lower jack → original MikroTik NAS bottom jack . Route both straight up the right rear post, through U15 and down to the MikroTik. Tie them near the tray and below U15 with slack.
