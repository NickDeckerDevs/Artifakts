How the cables are organized

Room cables · back of panel
  • House leads enter behind the left side
  • Office leads enter behind jacks 13–16
  • Support the bundle on the panel’s rear bar
  • Keep the room leads at the back
  • Use a short front jumper for each connected jack
Rack cables · through the brush
  • C1 · NAS upper cable
  • C2 · NAS lower cable
  • C3 · SmartThings cable
  • C4 · Gateway cable to the MikroTik
  • Bring these cables up the right rear side
  • Pass them forward through the U15 brush opening
  • Bring them down the front to their switch sockets
  • Support the cables with loose Velcro
Rack cables · no brush opening
  • C5 · Converter to gateway
  • Keep C5 behind the equipment
  • C6 · MikroTik to TP-Link
  • Keep C6 at the front
  • Incoming optical fiber stays at the back
