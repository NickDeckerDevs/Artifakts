Six matching colors

  • Yellow
    • 01–04 · Living area and Orbi branch
  • Orange
    • 05–08 · Second office and child’s room
  • White
    • 09–12 · Master bedroom and garage
  • Blue
    • 13–16 · Your office
  • Black
    • 17–20 · Rack equipment
  • Red
    • 21–24 · Internet service links
  • Match the front jumper color to its jack
  • Keep the existing room cable behind the panel
  • Put the same number label at both ends of each cable
  • Include PANEL or SWITCH on each numbered label
  • Keep reserved jacks disconnected
  • Fit four inserts of each color if the stock supports this allocation
  • Count the actual colors before fitting inserts
  • The matching short jumpers are 0.2 m long
  • Use a longer cable from your supply wherever a short jumper pulls on a jack
  • Color identifies a purpose; it does not create a separate network
  • The view uses numbered positions rather than a mechanical drawing of the staggered openings

What happened with the living-room testing

  • The November 11 survey put the PlayStation on MikroTik Ethernet 7
  • The TP-Link house switch was also on Ethernet 7
  • The recorder and both Orbi units were on Ethernet 1
  • The recorder’s exact physical socket was not established
  • The separate living-room drop is reported; its rack end is not matched
  • The second office needs one position plus an optional second
  • The child’s room needs one position plus an optional second
  • The master bedroom needs one position plus an optional second
  • The garage cable is reported; its rack end is not matched
  • These results predate the fiber switch-over

Routing behind and in front

  • House cables · enter the rear on the left
  • Office cables · enter the rear below 13–16
  • Rack equipment cables · enter the rear on the right
  • Support the rear cables on the panel’s support bar
  • Office jumpers · route upward to the MikroTik at U14
  • House jumpers · route downward to the original switch
  • Orbi branch · keep MikroTik Ethernet 1 as its destination
  • Rack jumpers · use the destination listed for that individual jack
  • Leave enough slack to reach every plug
  • Incoming optical fiber · stays outside this Ethernet panel
  • Do not move working cables during this review
