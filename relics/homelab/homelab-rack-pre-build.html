homelab rack pre build

Parts and measurements to have on the bench.

Use these parts

Rack and screws

Assembled ECHOGEAR EGAV-PROF15. Four leveling feet, one rack pad under each foot, and #10-32 ECHOGEAR rack screws. Set aside any M6 cage nuts for a rack with compatible square holes.

Three trays

Included 2U × 18-inch tray for the upright ASUSTOR NAS. Included 1U × 15.12-inch tray for the Comcast modem/router. One ordered IRONDIY 1U × 16-inch tray for the Samsung hub. Keep the second IRONDIY tray in reserve.

Front-mounted parts

Two Cable Matters 1U brush panels, Tecmojo 13110902 switched strip, MikroTik white ears, TP-Link Kit-13 ears, trueCABLE staggered 24-port panel, its rear bar, and the two GeeekPi insert and short-cable kits.

Power and cable routing

CyberPower CP1500 UPS, original device power cords and adapters, Velcro, labels, and longer Ethernet cables from your supply. Keep a short rated outlet saver handy if the SmartThings adapter blocks outlet 2.

Keep the separate Tecmojo four-post rails on the bench. Their listed minimum span is 20.9 inches, greater than this rack’s overall depth.

Measure as you dry-fit

  1. Rack. Measure total height with the pads and leveling feet. Available height is roughly 31–32 inches. Manufacturer drawing lists a footprint about 20.4 inches wide by 20 inches deep. Leave room for rear plugs and the coax bend.
  2. NAS shelf. Set it at U3–U4. NAS case is 6.85 × 6.69 × 9.06 inches. Check usable tray depth, the gap above the case, and the reach of the two Ethernet cables. Weigh the NAS with all drives installed before buying separate isolation pads.
  3. Comcast shelf. Set it at U8. Check the case and actual cable envelope with 2–3 inches of ventilation clearance. Reserve U9–U10 for that space.
  4. Hub shelf. Dry-fit the IRONDIY rear extension and sliding stop at U11 before putting the hub on it. Check that the plugs clear the rail and the device clears the switch above.
  5. Power strip. At U2, check rear plug height and finger access before placing the NAS above it. Check that the UPS cord and rack strip cord each reach a grounded wall socket.
